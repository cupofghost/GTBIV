## Master voice direction

Turbo has two registers and this file is almost entirely the second one.

**The street voice** (already recorded / already written): loud, fast, one-liner
machine, deflects everything. That's what he does at strangers, cars, and cops.
Do not use it here.

**The straight voice** (this file): slower. Fewer words. Sentences that stop
where they stop instead of turning into a joke. He is not performing, because
the people in these scenes have all known him too long for it to work. He is
often losing an argument and often not answering the question.

**Practical direction for the read:** take about 20% off his usual pace, let the
pauses sit, and resist the urge to land a button on the last word of a line.
Several of these lines are him running out of things to say — play that, don't
fill it.

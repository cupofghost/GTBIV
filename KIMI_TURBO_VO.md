## THE VOICE

**Turbo Jones.** Thirty-four. American, no strong regional placing — city, not
country, not coastal-posh. Mid-range male voice with a bit of grain in it.
Naturally fast and a little loud, because he has spent his whole life talking
his way out of rooms.

He just got out of minimum security. He owes his ex-wife child support for a
seven-year-old son, and there is a court date. He is not a tough guy and should
never be read as one. He is a charming man who is running out of road.

**The single most important note in this document:** Turbo has two deliveries,
and almost everything here is the second one.

### Delivery A — THE STREET VOICE
Loud, fast, one line long, always landing a joke at the end. This is what he
does at strangers, cars, and cops. **You have already recorded this** — it's the
existing `TURBO_LINES` material ("Borrowing this. Permanently." / "Eat my
exhaust!"). Use it as the baseline the listener already knows.

**No group in this file uses Delivery A.** It's described only so you have the
contrast in your ear — every one of the 239 lines below is Delivery B.

### Delivery B — THE STRAIGHT VOICE
Same man, no performance. Slower — take about 20% off his usual pace. Quieter.
Sentences stop where they stop instead of turning into a joke. He is usually
losing the conversation and often not answering the question he was asked.

**Three things that will make or break this whole session:**

1. **Do not land the last word.** His street voice puts a button on every line.
   These lines don't have one. Resist adding the lift — let the line just end.
2. **Let the pauses sit.** Ellipses and dashes are real hesitations, not
   stylistic. Several lines are him running out of things to say; play the
   running out, don't fill it.
3. **No self-awareness, ever.** He never sounds like a man who finds himself
   amusing. If a read gets a laugh in these groups, it's wrong — go flatter.

The gap between A and B is the entire character. The player has heard him
deflect for hours; these are the scenes where it stops working.
